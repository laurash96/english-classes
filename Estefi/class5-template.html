Interrupted
Four real moments from your life where something interrupted what you were doing. Write them here, then read two aloud and tell a third one from memory.
⏱ Writing time: 15 minutes
🎯 Goal: was/were + -ing → interrupted by a Past Simple action
✅ Real stories > invented ones
1
Every story needs two parts: what you were doing (the long background action) and what interrupted it (the short, sudden one).
2
Real beats invented. A phone call during a shower, the power cutting out mid-movie — the more specific, the easier it is to remember and retell.
3
